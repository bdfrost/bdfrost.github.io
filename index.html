About

The platform should be the easy part.

I'm a principal platform engineer. I've run large-scale infrastructure at Seagate, kept cloud-native e-commerce running at Techolution, and today I build the platform behind Wendy's mobile and web ordering.

My job is to take friction out of shipping. When the paved road is the easiest road, developers move faster and production gets more stable, not less. At Wendy's, that approach took us from months to hours to deploy new code, and from 98% availability to a level so high we no longer measure it below 99.999%. The platform runs on a tiny fraction of overall cloud spend while the revenue it carries keeps growing.

  • Paved roads beat gates and tickets.
  • Reliability is a feature that ships with every release.
  • Spend where it earns its keep.
  • Grow the next generation of principal engineers.

Never mistake activity for achievement.
